Welcome Home NA Group has moved

If you're looking for the Welcome Home meeting that used to be at 20th & Kipling, we've moved to 11500 W. 20th Ave., Lakewood, the church at the southeast corner of 20th & Simms.

Every Thursday at 7:00 p.m. Park in the lot off N. Robb St. and enter the building through the courtyard to the left of the main doors.

Welcome Home is a Narcotics Anonymous (NA) group meeting in Lakewood, Colorado, close to Wheat Ridge, Arvada, Golden and west Denver.

More NA resources

  • Mile High Area of NA: meeting list, events and a helpline for Denver and the metro area. Call or text (303) 832-3784 to talk with a recovering addict.
  • NA Colorado Region: find NA meetings anywhere in Colorado, plus newcomer resources and regional events.
  • Colorado Regional Convention of NA (CRCNA): the annual statewide NA convention in Denver.
  • Narcotics Anonymous World Services: NA literature, information for newcomers and meeting search worldwide.
